OTA Business Desktop 1.8.0.

## What's new
- **Export every Accounts sheet to Excel.** A new **Export…** button in Accounts covers every table: ledgers (one or many parties), bills & advices statements, vouchers, day book, trial balance, profit & loss, balance sheet, commission, ageing, trade outstanding, cash/bank book, buyers, suppliers, chart of accounts and audit log.
- **More export options.** Excel, CSV or PDF; one workbook with a sheet per report, a file per report, or a file per party. Choose the columns, and whether to include opening/closing balances, subtotals and the running balance.
- **Custom filters.** Period presets (FY, quarter, month, custom), several buyers/suppliers/accounts at once, account groups, voucher types (JV, PV, RV, CV, CM), amount range, Dr or Cr side, narration text, bill status (due, partly paid, paid) and hide zero balances.
- **Better Excel files.** Frozen header with a filter row, amounts as real numbers in ₹ with 2 decimals and Indian grouping, and bold totals.
- The test login exports only its test books; real exports never include test data.

Windows and Mac users get this version through the in-app update prompt. A backup of the local database is taken before installing. If you are on 1.2.1 or older, install this version once by hand.

**Mac: first start of a new install.** The app is not signed by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Choose *Done*, open *System Settings > Privacy & Security*, click **Open Anyway** next to OTA Business and confirm. Start it from Applications, not from the disk image.

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer is not code-signed: choose *More info* > *Run anyway*.
