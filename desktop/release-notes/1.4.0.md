OTA Business for Windows 1.4.0.

## What's new
- **Accounts**, a new section for the agency's own books, kept on this laptop only.
  - **Chart of accounts** with sensible defaults (cash, bank, capital, commission income, rent, salaries and so on). You can add, rename or deactivate accounts. Every buyer and supplier gets a ledger.
  - **Vouchers.** Journal, payment, receipt and contra entries with several Dr/Cr lines that must balance. You can edit or delete them, and the audit log records every change.
  - **Commission.** Set a % per supplier under *Parties & commission*. Each bill then posts its commission automatically (Dr supplier, Cr Commission income) and updates when the bill is edited or deleted, never twice. Changing a supplier's % re-posts all of that supplier's bills.
  - **Ledgers** for any account, buyer or supplier, with a date range, opening and running balance, and a click-through to the voucher or bill.
  - **Reports:** day book, trial balance, profit & loss, balance sheet, commission by supplier and month, party ageing, trade outstanding and cash/bank book. Export any of them to Excel or PDF.
- Accounts never go to the cloud and are never archived or purged. They are included in the automatic backup taken before each update.

This version installs through the in-app update prompt. If you are on 1.2.1 or older, install 1.3.0 or this version once by hand first.

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer is not code-signed: choose *More info* > *Run anyway*.
