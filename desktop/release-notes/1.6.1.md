OTA Business Desktop 1.6.1.

## What's new
- **Order and advice numbers are never reused.** A deleted order's number is not given out again: after 26270001–26270005 were deleted, the next order is 26270006. The cloud counters only move forward.
- **Number clashes fix themselves.** If an order or advice saved on this laptop finds its number already taken in the cloud (for example by an older app version), it gets a fresh number from the cloud, a new PDF, and you are told. Nothing is overwritten.
- Deletes made on a phone or another laptop are removed from this laptop at the next sync, including orders this laptop had already stored permanently. A retention purge still never deletes from the laptop.

**If you are on 1.3.0–1.5.x:** orders deleted on a phone while you were on those versions may still show on the laptop. After this update they are removed at the next sync when the cloud has their deletion; any left over can be deleted on the laptop and stay deleted everywhere.

Windows and Mac users get this version through the in-app update prompt. A backup of the local database is taken before installing. If you are on 1.2.1 or older, install this version once by hand.

**Mac: first start of a new install.** The app is not signed by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Choose *Done*, open *System Settings > Privacy & Security*, click **Open Anyway** next to OTA Business and confirm. Start it from Applications, not from the disk image.

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer is not code-signed: choose *More info* > *Run anyway*.
