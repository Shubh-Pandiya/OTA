Everything from 1.1.0 is included:
- **Updates:** on start and every few hours the app checks for a newer version and offers **Update now** or **Later**. Android asks once to allow installs from OTA Business ("Install unknown apps"). Sync Settings > App updates checks on demand.
- **Deletes reach every device:** an order, bill or advice deleted on any phone or the laptop is removed from all of them at their next sync.
- **Numbers are never reused:** order and advice numbers are never given out twice, even after deletes. If two devices ever end up with the same number, the second one gets a fresh number from the cloud.

**Phones on the first version (no updater yet):** install `OTA-Business-1.1.1.apk` once by hand, over the existing app. Do **not** uninstall first: installing over it keeps everything. If Android says the app isn't compatible or the package conflicts, the signing key differs; stop and tell the admin (nothing on the phone has changed).
