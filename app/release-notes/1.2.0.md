**This is the last version of this app. It moves your phone to the new OTA Business app.**

After updating, the app asks **Move to the new app?** Tap **Move now** (Wi-Fi or mobile data needed):
- It first uploads everything not yet synced, and won't continue until nothing is waiting.
- It downloads the new app and checks it is the genuine OTA release.
- It installs the new app next to this one. Android asks you to confirm the install.
- It copies your orders, bills, advices, to-dos, cheque pictures and settings into the new app, and keeps an encrypted safety copy in *Download/OTA Business*.

Then sign in once in the new app. This old app shows a recovery code and a **Remove this old app** button. Keep the code until you have checked the new app.

Also new: an optional **app lock** with a PIN or fingerprint (Sync Settings → Security), and the data on the phone is now stored **encrypted**.
